# Demo script (8 minutes)

> **Fictional prototype.** Take-home challenge for the role of AI Solution Engineer (Zug). Not an official AECOM document. All names and data are synthetic.

The plan's section 8 scenario, in template mode (works offline) or with Claude (`backend/.env`, see the README). Start from a fresh backend or `make demo-reset`, on the Operations page, window at least 1,000 px wide.

| Time | Advisory | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:45 | — | Show the intro panel, the timeline and the map. | "A fictional utility, 72 hours before a hurricane. Five forecasts arrive every 12 hours. The tool prepares decisions; people make them." |
| 0:45–1:45 | 1 (T-72, Prepare) | **Issue advisory 1.** Point at the track and cone, the risk colours, the ranked list. Open **Decision package**. | "Exposure from a 50-member ensemble; risk is failure probability times consequence. The rule engine already finds gaps: the risk-list review (P-4.1), generators, 6 fuelled against 9 at-risk pumping stations serving the treatment plant (P-4.3), and poor-condition substations in the top 10 (P-4.4). The situation report (P-7.1) is approved from its draft below, not as an item." |
| 1:45–2:30 | 2 (T-60) | **Issue advisory 2.** Show *What changed* and the rank arrows; open advisory 1's audit events. | "Everything I left undecided closed as *not decided*: the storm doesn't wait, and the audit trail shows it. The poor-condition list changes with the ranking." |
| 2:30–4:30 | 3 (T-48, Full activation) | **Issue advisory 3.** Timeline turns to Full activation. Scroll the risk list to **PS-09** (rank 19, up from 39) or click it on the map near the coast: explanation drawer. In the package, as **Logistics**, approve *Assign a portable generator to PS-09 (feeds WTP-01)*. As **Regulatory Liaison**, approve *Contact the hospitals fed by SUB-07 (HOSP-01); confirm on-site backup power…* (P-6.2: Bayport General has its own tested backup with 96 hours of fuel), then the regulator notification (P-7.2). The draft cites only clauses and assets from the findings; if it invents one, the template replaces it." |
| 4:30–5:15 | 4 (T-36) | **Issue advisory 4.** Show STG-02 (P-4.6, staging area floods), flood barriers (P-5.1), and P-7.2 satisfied. | "The landfall moved east again; the coastal staging yard now floods." |
