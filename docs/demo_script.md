# Demo script (8 minutes)

> **Fictional prototype.** Take-home challenge for the role of AI Solution Engineer (Zug). Not an official AECOM document. All names and data are synthetic.

The plan's section 8 scenario (playbook v3.3) with the CR-02 and CR-03 demo additions, in template mode (works offline) or with Claude (`backend/.env`, see the README). Start from a fresh backend or `make demo-reset`, on the **Storm map** page, window at least 1,000 px wide. Pick the role under *Deciding as* before each decision.

| Time | Advisory | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | — | **Storm map**: the synthetic coastline, assets coloured by risk band, hospitals, shelters and staging areas, the legend and the cone note. | "Muster, with a fictional utility, 72 hours before a hurricane. Five forecasts arrive every 12 hours. The tool prepares decisions; people make them." |
| 0:30–1:15 | 1 (T-72) | **Issue advisory 1.** Point at the track, its intensity dots and time labels, and the cone. Open **Operations**, **Decision package**: as **Director**, declare **Level 2** (prepare), which the package proposes. | "Exposure from a 50-member ensemble; the cone uses the same radii. Risk is failure probability times consequence. The rule engine already finds gaps: the risk-list review (P-4.1), 6 fuelled generators against more at-risk pumping stations serving the treatment plant (P-4.3), poor-condition substations in the top 10 (P-4.4)." |
| 1:15–1:45 | 2 (T-60) | **Issue advisory 2.** On the storm map the track shifts east (the previous track stays dotted). Show *What changed* and the rank arrows. | "Everything I left undecided closed as *not decided*: the storm doesn't wait, and the audit trail shows it." |
| 1:45–3:30 | 3 (T-48) | **Issue advisory 3**; the cone narrows over Bayport. As **Incident Commander**, declare **Level 1** (full activation). Click **PS-09** on the map: explanation drawer. In the package, as **Logistics**, approve *Assign a portable generator to PS-09 (feeds WTP-01)* (P-4.5). As **Regulatory Liaison**, approve *Contact the hospitals fed by SUB-07 (HOSP-01); confirm on-site backup power* (P-6.2), then the regulator notification (P-7.2). As **Incident Commander**, **override** the PS-14 inspection (P-4.2): "crew shortage, re-plan at next advisory". Show the draft situation report, click a clause chip, edit one line and **approve** it. | "Generators go to the pumping stations that keep the water plant running. The hospital relies on its own on-site power, so we confirm it is ready. Only the deterministic apply step writes state. An override is a deviation, so only the Incident Commander makes it, with a reason. The draft cites only clauses and assets from the findings; if it invents one, the template replaces it." |
| 3:30–3:55 | 3 | **Field tasks**, district **Bayport West**: the PS-09 generator task with its clause and who approved it. | "The field supervisor sees only approved work in their district and can only mark it done or blocked; a blocked note becomes a gap in the next package." |
| 3:55–4:10 | 3 | **Run trace**, advisory 3: the waterfall of cycle steps and the pauses for decisions. | "What the agent did and what it cost: in template mode, zero tokens and zero cost; with Claude, tokens, cost, retries and any template fallback per step." |
| 4:10–5:00 | 4 (T-36) | **Issue advisory 4.** STG-02 floods (P-4.6); flood barriers (P-5.1): as **Logistics**, approve two barriers. **ICS-215 and IAP**: **Redraft from the current decisions**; the worksheet lists the PS-09 generator and the barriers with required, have and need. **Approve as Planning Section Chief**, then **Release as Incident Commander**. | "The ICS-215 and IAP objectives come from approved items only; code writes the rows, a model may only reword objectives. Nothing is released without both approvals." |
| 5:00–5:50 | 5 (T-24) | **Issue advisory 5.** In **Conflicts**: PS-14 citing P-4.2, P-4.7 and P-8.2. Try to defer it as **Operations**: refused. As **Incident Commander**, **Defer…** with "stop-work in force; inspect after landfall". | "Tropical-storm wind reaches PS-14 within 12 hours: the inspection we deferred now collides with stop-work. The tool routes it to the Incident Commander; personnel safety prevails." |
| 5:50–6:15 | 5 | **Copilot**: "Why is PS-14 ranked where it is?", click the PS-14 chip; "Will PS-14 fail?". | "Answers come from read-only tools and cite only what they returned. Asked for a prediction, it says nobody can say and gives the modelled probability." |
| 6:15–7:00 | — | **Calibration**, as **Planning**: **Run the back-test** on Storm Delta; both reliability curves, Brier score before and after. Then **Champion and challenger**: held-out Storm Delta metrics, the promotion rule, the disagreements list. | "Calibration is a proposed model version that a person approves. The challenger, trained on 20 synthetic storms, beats the rules on held-out Storm Delta and may be proposed, but it runs in shadow: the rules stay champion until SGW's own outcomes justify promotion and a person approves it." |
| 7:00–7:30 | — | **Audit**: filter **People**, then **Agent**. **Data quality**: the two rejected rows. | "Every step, decision and model call is recorded, with versions and an input hash. Bad data is rejected at the door." |
| 7:30–8:00 | — | Technical: `docs/eval/report.md` (golden set, five categories, gates) and `./scripts/check.sh` running the template eval gate. | "The AI is evaluated, not trusted: 30 golden cases, rule-based checks, and a gate that fails the build. A broken prompt fails it." |

**Alternative path** (also tested): at advisory 3, as **Operations**, *approve* the PS-14 inspection instead of overriding it (and, if you like, mark it **Done** in **Field tasks**). At advisory 5 there is no PS-14 conflict.
